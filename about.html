About

A little context behind the work, the questions I keep returning to, and the path that brought me here.

“I want to understand how intelligence works — and build things that help us ask better questions.”

My work brings together biochemistry, computer science and computational neuroscience. I move between biological questions and computational tools, with a continuing interest in how ideas travel from research into the wider world.

I'm especially interested in biologically constrained neural networks, connectome-based modelling, spatial representations, neuroimaging and the relationship between brains and artificial systems.

EDUCATION B.Sc. Biochemistry · HND Software Engineering · B.Sc. Computer Science Three complementary foundations across biological science, software engineering and computing.
RESEARCH African Neuro-Data Research Lab Computational neuroscience researcher working with neuroimaging and computational models, with a focus on brain aging and neurodegeneration.
