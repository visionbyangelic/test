About.

A little context behind the work, the questions I keep returning to, and the interdisciplinary path that brought me here.

● Computational Neuroscience Biochemistry & CS Embodied Intelligence Science Journalism

"I want to understand how intelligence works — and build things that help us ask better questions."

My work sits at the convergence of biochemistry, computer science, and computational neuroscience. Having trained across wet-lab biological systems and software engineering, I examine biological questions through biophysical models and physical simulations.

I'm deeply interested in biologically constrained neural networks, connectome-based emulation (Drosophila connectomics), spatial representations in place-cell populations, and translating complex primary literature into rigorous, accessible narratives.
