A LITTLE ABOUT ME

THE PERSON, NOT THE PROJECTS

Hi, I'm Angelic.
I’m endlessly curious.

I’m someone who likes pulling ideas apart to understand how they work — whether that’s a biological system, a piece of software, or a question about the mind.

I studied biochemistry before moving deeper into computer science and software engineering. Somewhere along that path, computational neuroscience became a way to bring those interests together. I enjoy learning across disciplines, making things, and following questions further than I initially intended.

Outside the technical side of things, I read, write, explore ideas, and collect little interests that can turn into surprisingly deep rabbit holes.
