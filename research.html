Connectome-to-Behavior: Closed-Loop Drosophila Emulation

FlyWire Connectome BANC Dataset Brian2 Biophysics MuJoCo Physics flygym Body Closed-Loop Simulation

Closed-loop simulation coupling complete Drosophila connectomes (FlyWire, BANC) with Brian2 point-neuron dynamics and an embodied biomechanical body (MuJoCo, flygym) to evaluate emergent locomotion, grooming, and feeding without hand-coded motor rules.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can natural coordinated behaviors emerge organically when real synaptic wiring is given simple biophysical rules and placed in an embodied physical simulation?

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Take the full wiring diagram of a fruit fly's brain, switch it on inside a computer, give it a virtual body, and see whether it walks, cleans itself and feeds, without anyone programming those moves.

COMPLETED RESEARCH ~80× SPATIAL INFO / SPIKE

EM-NAV: Geometry & Transferability of Spatial Representations

Spiking Neural Networks Place Cells Metabolic Sparsity Zero-Shot Transfer Representation Geometry
WATCH DEMONSTRATION VIDEO ↗

Computational study examining how biological constraints (spiking thresholds, metabolic sparsity penalties, and recurrence) shape internal spatial representations, place-cell-like geometry, and zero-shot transfer stability in embodied agents under severe sensory ambiguity.

KEY FINDING

Constrained spiking networks produced place-cell-like coding with up to ~80× higher spatial information per spike than unconstrained baselines, with stronger transfer into continuous 3D environments.

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Give a virtual explorer a brain that must save energy and only fire when it counts, like a real one. Its inner "map" starts to look like the place cells in real brains, and it copes better when dropped somewhere new.

Brain-Age Estimation from Structural MRI

Structural MRI Morphometrics OpenBHB Cohort OASIS-3 Dataset Biomarker Modeling

Structural MRI morphometrics across OpenBHB and OASIS-3 cohorts. Models healthy brain volume trajectories across the lifespan to quantify the biological-versus-chronological age gap as a predictive health biomarker.

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Teach a computer what a healthy brain looks like at each age from MRI scans. Then show it someone new: if their brain looks older than their birthday says, that gap may be a clue to health.
